<h1>🚀 DivinityEconomy 4.0.0 (26.2) 🚀</h1>
<h2>⚠️ Upgrade Notes ⚠️</h2>
<ul>
    <li><b>Java 25 is now required.</b> The plugin is compiled for Java 25 and will not load on older runtimes.</li>
    <li><b>Minecraft 26.2 is required.</b> This release targets the 26.2 API.</li>
    <li><b>Regenerate or update your <code>config.yml</code>.</b> The config format has changed: each market (Materials, Potions, Entities, Enchants, Experience) now has its own <code>Enable</code>, <code>Base Quantity</code>, <code>Buy/Sell Scaling</code> and <code>Pricing Model</code> settings. Back up your existing file, then let the plugin update it or delete it to regenerate, and re-apply your customisations.</li>
</ul>
<h2>➕ Features & Updates ➕</h2>
<ul>
    <li>
        <h4>New Pricing Model System</h4>
        <p>You can now choose between four different pricing models for your server's economy:
            <ul>
                <li><b>V1 (Linear):</b> The original pricing model with a simple inverse relationship between supply and price.</li>
                <li><b>V2 (Elasticity):</b> An improved model using logarithmic supply-demand curves for more realistic price movements. This is the recommended model for most servers.</li>
                <li><b>STATIC (Fixed):</b> Prices remain constant regardless of supply and demand. This is useful for servers that want stable, predictable pricing.</li>
                <li><b>STATIC_BOTTOMLESS (Fixed, Infinite Supply):</b> Like STATIC, prices stay constant, but quantities are <i>not</i> updated when players buy or sell. The market has unlimited supply and demand, so stock never runs out or builds up. Ideal for servers that want fully stable pricing with unlimited availability.</li>
            </ul>
        </p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Granular Market Configuration</h4>
        <p>The markets for Potions and Entities can now be configured separately from Materials. Each market (Materials, Potions, Entities, Enchants, Experience) has its own settings for <code>Enable</code>, <code>Base Quantity</code>, <code>Buy/Sell Scaling</code>, and <code>Pricing Model</code> in the <code>config.yml</code>.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Direct Price Setting with <code>/setprice</code></h4>
        <p>A new command alias <code>/setprice</code> (for <code>/setvalue</code>) has been added. When using the <code>STATIC</code> pricing model, this command now directly sets the fixed price for an item, rather than adjusting its stock quantity.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Balanced Experience Market</h4>
        <p>The buy and sell scaling for the experience market has been re-balanced. You may need to regenerate your <code>config.yml</code> to see these changes (<code>Buy Scaling: 1.25</code>, <code>Sell Scaling: 1.20</code>).</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Updated to Minecraft 26.2</h4>
        <p>DivinityEconomy is now fully compatible with Minecraft version 26.2.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>New Market Items (1.21.11 &ndash; 26.2)</h4>
        <p>The markets have been brought fully up to date with Minecraft 26.2. Only items players can actually obtain were added (technical blocks such as wall signs, crops and fluids are intentionally excluded):
            <ul>
                <li><b>26.2 Sulfur Caves:</b> Sulfur, Potent Sulfur and Cinnabar, each with polished, brick, chiseled, stair, slab and wall variants, plus Sulfur Spike, Bucket of Sulfur Cube, Music Disc "Bounce" and the Sulfur Cube spawn egg/spawner.</li>
                <li><b>Spears &amp; Nautilus Armor:</b> Wooden through Netherite Spears, Copper/Iron/Golden/Diamond/Netherite Nautilus Armor, Copper and Netherite Horse Armor, plus the Nautilus, Zombie Nautilus, Parched and Camel Husk spawn eggs/spawners.</li>
                <li><b>Copper Age:</b> Copper tools &amp; armor, Copper Nuggets, Copper Torch, and the full oxidised and waxed ranges of Chiseled Copper, Copper Bars, Chains, Doors, Trapdoors, Lanterns, Grates, Bulbs, Chests, Golem Statues and Lightning Rods. Also Iron Chain and Tuff slabs, stairs, walls and chiselled variants.</li>
                <li><b>Garden &amp; Wood:</b> Shelves (every wood type), Pale Oak extras, Pale Moss Block, Pale Hanging Moss, Bush, Firefly Bush, Dry Grass, Wildflowers, Leaf Litter, Cactus Flower, Golden Dandelion.</li>
                <li><b>Misc:</b> Happy Ghast Harnesses (all colours), Dried Ghast, Wolf Armor, coloured Bundles, Blue/Brown Eggs, Resin Brick, Chiseled Resin Bricks, new Banner Patterns, Armor Trims, Pottery Sherds and the "Tears", "Creator (Music Box)" and "Lava Chicken" music discs.</li>
                <li><b>Enchants:</b> Lunge (the new spear enchantment).</li>
                <li>All new spawn eggs and spawners are disabled by default, like all other spawners/spawn eggs - enable and price them via <code>config.yml</code>/<code>entities.yml</code>/<code>materials.yml</code> as needed.</li>
            </ul>
        </p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Aliases for All New Items</h4>
        <p>Every newly added item, spawner and the Lunge enchant has aliases, so players can type natural names such as <code>/buy oakshelf</code>, <code>/buy coppergolemstatue</code> or <code>/value sulfurcubecage</code>. Duplicate or conflicting aliases from earlier versions (such as repeated Relic disc and Breeze entries) have also been cleaned up.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Automated Test Suite</h4>
        <p>The project now includes an automated test suite covering commands, the pricing models and the experience market, to help catch regressions in future releases.</p>
    </li>

<h2>🪲 Bug Fixes 🪲</h2>
<ul>
    <li>
        <h4>Static means Static</h4>
        <p>Fixed an issue whereby disabling dynamic prices (by using the STATIC model) could still produce dynamic results.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed Invalid Potion &amp; Entity Entries</h4>
        <p>Healing, Harming, Leaping, Regeneration and Swiftness potions (and their splash, lingering and tipped arrow forms) used outdated potion type names that no longer exist and could not load. The Mooshroom and Snow Golem spawners had the same problem with entity names. All have been corrected.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed Invalid Material Entries</h4>
        <p>Replaced <code>SHELF</code>, <code>HANGING_PALE_MOSS</code> and <code>GHASTLING_SPAWN_EGG</code> with their correct material names (<code>*_SHELF</code>, <code>PALE_HANGING_MOSS</code>); Ghastlings have no spawn egg.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed <code>/evalue</code> for Non-Sword Enchants</h4>
        <p><code>/evalue</code> always valued enchants against a diamond sword, so enchants that don't apply to swords (such as Lunge, Efficiency or Power) reported "not supported on this item". It now reports the correct buy and sell value for every enchant. A related bug that showed the buy error in place of the sell error has also been fixed.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed Shutdown Errors</h4>
        <p>Addressed errors that occurred during server shutdown, ensuring a smoother and error-free shutdown process.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed Command Permission Messages</h4>
        <p>Corrected a typo (<code>permission-messaage</code>) in <code>plugin.yml</code> that stopped the no-permission message from being applied to the enchant and experience commands.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <h4>Fixed <code>/esellall</code> Permission</h4>
        <p>The <code>de.enchant.eSellAll</code> permission is now properly declared (default: true) and included in the enchant permission group.</p>
    </li>
</ul>
